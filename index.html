Why Play a Card Game?

Memory games are simple, fun and good for your brain. Yes, even better than scrolling for 4 hours.

  • It makes your memory stronger.
  • It helps you focus (just for a bit).
  • It is a nice break from study and work.
  • It is fun for kids, parents and even grandparents.
  • It makes you feel like a genius when you win.

How to Play

Don't worry, it is not rocket science.

  1. Go to the Play page and pick a theme you like.
  2. Choose a difficulty. Be honest with yourself.
  3. Press Start and the cards will appear.
  4. Click two cards. If they match, great. If not, they flip back.
  5. Find all pairs to win. Brag to your friends. Repeat.
